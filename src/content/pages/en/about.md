## Short Bio

I am a PhD candidate in **Management Science and Engineering** at the
[Institutes of Science and Development, Chinese Academy of Sciences][casisd].
My research focuses on **decision analysis, risk modeling, and resilience
assessment**, with an emphasis on practical methods for managing risks in
critical infrastructure and emergency material support systems.

Alongside my research, I enjoy programming and software development. I build
practical tools, explore data- and intelligence-driven methods, and contribute
to open-source projects. I also share research notes, technical resources, and
tutorials on my [blog](https://waynexucn.github.io/blog/).

I am open to academic and technical collaboration. Please feel free to [get in touch](mailto:wenjie.xu.cn@outlook.com).
